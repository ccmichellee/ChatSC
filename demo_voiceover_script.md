# ChatSC Demo Voiceover Script

**Duration**: ~60–75 seconds  
**Tone**: Conversational, confident, energetic — spoken like a USC student showing a working product to fellow engineers and judges.

---

### `0:00 – 0:16` | Beat 1: The Proactive Hook
* **On Screen**: Phone or Mac screen showing Messages. An incoming proactive text from Tommy pops up without prior prompting.
* **Transcript on Screen**:
  > *Tommy: "Hey Gabriel, quick heads-up — you're clear for two big ones that match your AI/systems + SWE internship interests! Google on Wed, Nov 18, 6-8pm in SGM 123 (distributed systems, interview Q&A, boba + pizza) and Meta on Tue, Nov 24, 5:30-7:30pm in RTH 105 (foundation models/PyTorch, 2027 intern networking, Sweetgreen dinner). Fight On! ✌️"*
* **Voiceover**:  
  > "College campuses bury everything in separate portals — EngageSC for clubs, Handshake for recruiting, and university calendars for events. Most students miss out simply because they don't check ten websites every day.  
  >  
  > Meet Tommy. He lives in your native iMessage, reads your schedule, and reaches out first. Right here, Tommy pinged me about Google and Meta tech talks that match my CS profile — and already checked my calendar to make sure I'm free."

---

## Recording Tips

1. **Pacing**: Aim for around 130–140 words per minute. Allow slight pauses right before message bubbles arrive so the viewer's eyes register the text as you speak about it.
2. **Audio Setup**: Use headphones with a mic or a desktop USB microphone in a quiet room with minimal echo.
3. **Delivery**: Speak naturally with conversational cadence, keeping energy steady and confident.
