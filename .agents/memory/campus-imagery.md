---
name: Campus photography
description: Requirement for authentic campus imagery in ChatSC.
---

Use authentic campus photographs from uploads or freely licensed sources, not
AI-generated imagery, for ChatSC's campus hero.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected the generated campus hero and asked for a
real USC photograph from a legitimate reusable source.

**How to apply:** Verify the image's subject, source, and license before use, and
keep the required credit and license link visible with the photograph.
